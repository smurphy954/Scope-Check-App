Welcome

Built for the elements. Designed for the eye.

Every cabinet line in this book has been selected for one reason: it has to look beautiful and stay beautiful, year after year, in real outdoor conditions — sun, salt air, humidity, rain, and the occasional hurricane season.

What separates these collections is how they get there: marine-grade polymer, powder-coated stainless steel, acetylated wood, weatherproof polyurethane resin, or compact phenolic board. Each material has a personality. Each finish opens up a different design direction.

The pages that follow walk through every collection — what it's made of, how it looks, and where it shines — so you can choose the language for your outdoor kitchen with confidence.

Danver · Overview

Stainless steel, dressed for the outdoors.

Danver pioneered the powder-coated stainless steel outdoor cabinet. Every box is fully welded stainless, then painted on every face before assembly — so the steel is never directly exposed to the environment.

The result: a cabinet that won't rust, won't rot, won't warp, and can be matched to virtually any architectural palette.

Why Danver

  • Built-to-order in Wallingford, CT — not a stock SKU.
  • 13 powder-coat colors plus 9 realistic wood-grain finishes.
  • Soft-close doors and drawers, magnets and gaskets on every door for high-wind / rooftop installs.
  • Fully integrates grills, refrigeration, sinks, and trash — designed around premium appliances.
  • Two flagship collections in this book: Urbane and Cosmopolitan.

Cosmopolitan

Also designed in collaboration with Daniel Germani, Cosmopolitan is the minimalist counterpart to Urbane — an aluminum-framed, slat-front cabinet that reads more like outdoor furniture than traditional casework. The architecture is the appliance.

Construction

Powder-coated stainless steel storage and drawer boxes hung from a Midnight Matte powder-coated aluminum frame. Boxes can mount from the top rail or sit on a lower shelf — reconfigurable as the kitchen evolves.

Finishes

  • Frame: Midnight Matte (standard)
  • Slats: any Danver powder-coat color, including all wood-grain options.
  • Drawer interior: Walnut wood-grain (standard) or matched to slat selection.

Why It Works Outdoors

  • Aluminum frame — no rust risk, even at the coast.
  • Open architecture lets air move — reduces humidity build-up.
  • Modular: add or remove storage modules without rebuilding the run.
Fresh, minimalist, all-weather.

Elements by TECNO

Elements is Brown Jordan's modular, furniture-inspired line — another Daniel Germani collaboration. Where TECNO is one continuous architectural plane, Elements is a kit of parts: discrete cooking, prep, storage, and serving modules that can be combined to fit any footprint.

Modular Approach

Each element is a finished, free-standing module. Lay them in line for a long galley, wrap them around a corner, or float a single island for a small terrace — the kit is the design system.

Materials & Finish

  • Powder-coated stainless steel construction (TECNO platform).
  • Choice of finish across modules — mix neutrals and accents.
  • Optional Dekton tops for integrated work surfaces.

Why Specifiers Choose It

  • Fits irregular or compact terraces where built-ins won't.
  • Re-configurable later — modules can move.
  • Reads as outdoor furniture; not "casework planted outside."
Furniture-grade modularity, kitchen-grade performance.

NatureKast

The first range of 100% weatherproof outdoor cabinets that look and feel like a high-end indoor kitchen. Realistic wood, stone, brick, and coral panels — molded in marine-grade resin.

Cabinet Lookbook10 · NatureKast
NatureKast · Collection

Indoor warmth, outdoor durability.

NatureKast doors and panels are molded in high-density polyurethane resin and hand-finished to look and feel like real wood. Euro doors and panels use high-pressure phenolic laminate. The result: details that have only ever been available indoors — corbels, trim, crown moldings, real wood-grain — now rated for full-time outdoor use.

Door Styles

  • Slab — clean, modern, flat panel.
  • Shaker — classic five-piece frame and panel.
  • Rustique — reclaimed-wood character with visible grain.
  • Louver — horizontal slat detailing for coastal looks.
  • Contempo — minimalist contemporary profile.
  • Euro — high-pressure phenolic laminate, ultra-flat.

Panel Looks Beyond Wood

  • Stack stone · Brick · Coral stone (molded panels).
  • Matching corbels, trim, and crown moldings.

Cabinet Range

Base, grill, insert, ceramic-grill, bench, hood, wall, and tall cabinets — a complete range, not just a few SKUs.

The same material specified for laboratory casework and high-traffic public restrooms, finished here for residential outdoor kitchens.

Collection 05

Windswept Collection

For projects that want the warmth of real wood outdoors. Windswept doors are made from Accoya — radiata pine sourced and processed in the Netherlands, then modified through a process called acetylation that converts it into an extremely stable, weatherproof lumber.

The Material

Acetylation chemically modifies the wood at a cellular level — the result is a board that resists rot, fungal attack, and dimensional change due to moisture. It is genuine timber that performs like a polymer.

Door Styles

  • Shaker — classic five-piece construction with a floating center panel; engineered so any small natural variance in the wood will not warp or distort the door.
  • Louver — the most intricate option, with overlapping horizontal slats.
  • Slab — cleanest and most minimal.

Why It's Specified

  • True wood look and feel — not a print or a faux finish.
  • Holds finish beautifully; can be stained or painted.
  • Backed by a 10-year warranty on the doors.
Real timber. Built to stay still.

HDPE Marine Grade Collection

100% American-made starboard cabinetry — the same marine-grade polymer used to build boat consoles, dock boxes, and pool deck furniture. When the brief is "set it and forget it," this is the cabinet.

The Material

High-density polyethylene — a marine-grade polymer that won't mold, won't rust, won't rot, and won't retain harmful bacteria. Where many outdoor kitchens use a metal frame and live with the inevitable possibility of corrosion, an HDPE cabinet simply doesn't go there.

Why South Florida Owners Choose It

  • Built to hold up to salt air, hurricane-season humidity, and direct sun.
  • Maintenance-free — no refinishing, no resealing, no rust touch-ups.
  • Maximum interior storage — no metal frame consuming wall thickness.
  • Hose it down and it looks new.

Quality & Warranty

  • 100% American-made.
  • Manufacturer's limited lifetime warranty.
  • State-of-the-art CNC fabrication.
If the boats can live in it, your kitchen can too.

Next Steps

How we move from book to build.

1 · Shortlist

Pick two or three collections from this book that speak to you. We'll bring physical samples of finishes and door fronts for the ones you like.

2 · Layout & Appliances

We confirm the kitchen footprint, appliances (grill, refrigeration, sink, ventilation), and any integration points — pergola, countertops, hardscape.

3 · Renderings & Pricing

You'll receive a final 3D rendering, a detailed specification sheet, and a fixed-price proposal for the selected collection.

4 · Build

Cabinets are built to order. Lead times vary by collection — we'll confirm a delivery and install window with the proposal.

What We Need From You

  • Confirmed appliance package (or budget for us to spec one).
  • Site access for measurements.
  • Finish direction — warm vs. cool, neutral vs. statement.
